## 6. Long Description (Markdown — paste 1:1)

# 🎵 BeatByte — the music bot your server deserves

**Completely free. Zero setup.** Rythm and Groove are gone, and voice channels have been way too quiet since. BeatByte fills that gap — a full-featured music bot that gets your server playing in seconds. Just type `/play`, drop a song name or a link, and the music starts.

*Part of the **ByteBots** family: no setup marathon, no paywall, no premium locks.*

## 🚀 Why BeatByte?

- 💸 **100% free** — no premium, no paywalls, no hidden limits.
- 🎧 **Everything in one bot** — every source, filters, Auto-DJ, playlists and a Web Player.
- ⚡ **Instant** — from search to sound in seconds, no config.

## 🔊 Play from anywhere

One command, every source. **`/play`** takes a search term or a direct link — single tracks or full playlists — from **YouTube**, **Spotify**, **Apple Music** and **Deezer**.

## 🎚️ Shape your sound

Dial in the vibe with **`/filter`** — **Bassboost**, **Nightcore**, **Slowed** and a **custom EQ** — and set the level with **`/volume`** (0–200%).

## 🤖 Auto-DJ, Now Playing & Lyrics

- 🔁 **`/autodj`** keeps the music going with similar tracks when the queue runs empty. Never any silence.
- 📺 **`/nowplaying`** shows a **live progress bar** and **album art**, so you always see what's on and how long is left.
- 📝 **`/lyrics`** pulls up the lyrics for the current or any song — for the 3 a.m. karaoke moment.

## 📋 A queue that thinks ahead

The queue **preloads** upcoming tracks, so **`/skip`** is noticeably faster — no buffering. Reorder and manage it with `/queue`, `/shuffle`, `/loop`, `/move`, `/remove`, `/clear`, `/position` and `/playnow`. **Skip votes** keep skipping fair for everyone, and an optional **DJ role** (`/setrole`) lets trusted members skip on their own.

## 💾 Playlists & 🌐 Web Player

Save your sets with **`/playlist`** — `save`, `load`, `list` and `show`. Prefer clicking to typing? Open the browser **Web Player** with **`/app`** and control everything after "Sign in with Discord" at **[beatbyte.bytebots.de](https://beatbyte.bytebots.de)**.

## ✨ Well-behaved

BeatByte respects a kick from the channel and **auto-leaves when nobody's left** — no idle bot hanging around.
