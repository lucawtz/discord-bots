## 2. Long Description (Markdown)

# 👋 Welcome to ByteBots

This is the **official support and community server for ByteBots** — the home of our free Discord bots. Whether something isn't working, you have an idea, or you just want to keep up with what's new: you're in the right place.

**ByteBots** builds professional, genuinely free Discord bots — no paywalls, no premium locks, no setup marathons. Every bot speaks German and comes with its own web app you control right from your browser.

## 🤖 The bots

- 🎵 **BeatByte** — a full-featured music bot. Play from **YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music & Deezer** with Auto-DJ, audio filters, playlists and a browser Web Player. Just type `/play`.
- 🔊 **EarTastic** — an interactive soundboard. Fire your own uploaded clips or effects from Freesound, organize everything into categories and favorites, and manage it all from a web dashboard.
- ➕ **More coming soon** — the ByteBots lineup keeps growing. Moderation, mini-games and server utilities are next, and this is where they'll launch first.

*Tell us which bot you'd like to see — feature requests here shape what we build.*

## 💬 What you get here

- 🛟 **Direct support** — ask a question and get help straight from the developer, not a ticket queue that never answers.
- 🐛 **Report bugs** — found something broken? Tell us and we'll fix it fast.
- 💡 **Request features & vote** — help decide what gets built next. This is where the roadmap actually comes from.
- 📢 **Announcements & changelog** — be the first to know about new features, bots and updates.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Community** — meet other server owners using ByteBots and share tips.

## 🚀 Get started

Invite a bot, join a voice channel and type `/play` (BeatByte) or `/soundboard` (EarTastic) — that's it. No config, no privileged intents, nothing to pay.

🌐 **Website:** https://bytebots.de
🎵 **BeatByte Web Player:** https://beatbyte.bytebots.de

**Pull up a chair — we're glad you're here.** 🎶
